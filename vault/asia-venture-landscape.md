# Asia Venture Landscape

Links: [[index]], [[hsbc-ventures-model]], [[future-of-work]], [[specialism-decision]], [[core-rules]].

## Market view (from the user, 2026-10-24; to verify with data)
- **China is where the real deals are:** a big market and a huge AI sector.
- **Singapore is second,** acting as the hub for SEA as a region.
- **Thailand is weak** as a deal market. Being Thai is not a strong sourcing advantage.

## Implications
- **Mandarin unlocks the main market.** Under [[core-rules]] it's a soft blocker. If the investing track becomes the direction, Mandarin is a high-value multi-year commitment.
- **English-only access means Singapore/SEA.** That's the realistic market for deals in the near term.
- Bangkok is a weak base for venture work. Keep it only as a lifestyle option.
