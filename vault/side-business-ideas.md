# Side Business Ideas

Links: [[index]], [[persona]], [[founder-mindset]], [[career-direction]].

## Constraints and criteria
- Time: 5-10 hours a week.
- Risk: moderate, calculated bets.
- Form: undecided between freelance or consulting, software product, and content or teaching.
- Should use skills I already have: SQL, Python, Excel VBA, data analysis, AI tools.
- Interests to draw from: game development, investing and market analysis, cooking, martial arts and fitness.
- Open to market gaps, not only passion projects.

## Candidate hypotheses (untested)
These are starting points, not recommendations. Each needs validation.

1. **Automation for small businesses.** Excel VBA and SQL automation for SMEs that still run on spreadsheets. Test: find five businesses with a recurring manual report and ask whether they would pay.
2. **Data and AI consulting for local firms.** Fixed-scope projects, such as a reporting pipeline or an LLM assistant on internal documents. Test: offer one discounted pilot.
3. **Indie game tooling or a small game.** Connects to game development. Test: a playable prototype in two weeks, with a feedback check.
4. **Market-analysis content or tools.** Connects to investing and market analysis. Test: a published weekly analysis for six weeks, then measure the audience.

## Validation method
- For each idea, define the signal that would justify continuing, and the signal that would kill it.
- Use the [[founder-mindset]] loop: a small test within one week, then a review.
