## Strategy
- **No career in FDE.** It disappears within a few years ([[ai-vision]]). Only take FDE work now as a hybrid with product, purely to get a raise or promotion soon. Never pitch myself as an FDE.
- **Win on the [[core-rules]] levers:** hands-on experience, deep specialisation, better execution. Every project passes the impact test (problem, users, quantified change).
- **Product management is the core skill** to build alongside the domain.
- Soft blockers (Mandarin, deal experience, maths) are costs with timelines, not exclusions.
- **Market evidence ([[future-of-work]]):** score each specialism on the four survival traits (judgment, accountability, domain plus AI, relationships). Move out of junior-looking, information-processing work while my experience edge lasts.

## Decision criteria
- Fit with [[ai-vision]]: business-side and specialist, not engineering.
- Builds toward the durable stack: accountability + ownership + trust + scarce context ([[ai-maturity-jobs]]).
- Pay and upside.
- Depth of specialisation it builds.
- Hands-on building plus client-facing work.
- Hard blockers: any present rules a path out; soft ones only add time.
- Financial runway for any move with uncertain income.
