# Learning Plans: AI PM and Deal Execution

Links: [[index]], [[action-plan]], [[skill-product-management]], [[hsbc-ventures-model]], [[core-rules]], [[ai-maturity-jobs]].

**Principle:** learn by producing real work ([[core-rules]]: hands-on beats credentials). Every module ends in an artefact that is useful at work or shows judgment. Each track gets 1.5 h/week (see the time template in [[action-plan]]).

## Track 1: AI product management (Nov 2026 to Jan 2027)
| Weeks | Module | Learn from | Output (real work) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Discovery: framing problems, user interviews | *Continuous Discovery Habits* (Teresa Torres) | Interview 3 investors about their AI pain points (also serves the self-sufficiency programme) |
| 3-4 | Product specs and prioritisation | *Inspired* (Marty Cagan); Lenny's Newsletter PRD examples | One-page product spec for the investor self-sufficiency programme |
| 5-6 | Metrics and outcomes | Lenny's Newsletter / podcast on metrics | 3 success KPIs for the programme, with a baseline measured |
| 7-8 | AI-specific: evals, quality, cost, guardrails, human-in-the-loop | *AI Engineering* (Chip Huyen), eval chapters | Eval plan for the agent I shipped (I already have evals; formalise them) |
| 9-10 | AI product strategy: build vs buy, model choice, risk in a bank | Vendor docs, HSBC AI policies | A build-vs-buy memo for one investor workflow |
| 11-12 | Product sense practice | Product-sense question sets | 4 written answers, critiqued with Claude; update [[skill-product-management]] |

**Proof by end Jan 2027:** spec, KPIs and eval plan in hand, all usable in the sponsor/role conversation and on the CV.

## Track 2: Deal execution (Nov 2026 to Jan 2027)
| Weeks | Module | Learn from | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | How VC deals work: term sheets, rounds, cap tables | *Venture Deals* (Feld & Mendelson) | Glossary plus a one-page term sheet explainer |
| 3-4 | CVC specifics: strategic value, POCs, sponsorship | Past HSBC Ventures investment memos (I've reviewed them for regulatory checks) | Pattern note: what makes HSBC memos pass, added to [[hsbc-ventures-model]] |
| 5-6 | Startup valuation and returns | *Venture Deals*; VC method / comps | Excel cap table + exit waterfall model (plays to my VBA/modelling strength) |
| 7-8 | Due diligence: commercial, tech, team, legal | VC diligence checklists | My own AI-startup tech diligence checklist (tests the AI-diligence edge) |
| 9-10 | Investment memo writing | Past HSBC memos as templates | Monthly thesis written as a full mock memo |
| 11-12 | Live exposure | Ask the sponsor or a Ventures director | Sit in on or support diligence on 1 live deal |

**Proof by end Jan 2027:** a cap table model, an AI-diligence checklist and a mock memo, plus one live-deal exposure.

## After week 12
- Review both tracks at the quarterly review (2027-01-01) and decide where to go deeper.
- Track 1 next: own a real product metric. Track 2 next: regular diligence support on deals.
