# Review Cadence

Links: [[index]], [[action-plan]], [[coaching-approach]], [[future-timeline]], [[decision-log]], [[coach-learnings]].

Scheduled tasks prepare a draft of each review in `vault/reviews/`. Then we talk it through, and I update the vault.

| Review | When (HK time) | Covers | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | Sat 12:00 | Last week's progress; day-by-day actions for the coming week; timeline status vs [[action-plan]]; new thoughts | `reviews/weekly/YYYY-Www.md` |
| **Monthly** | 1st of month, 10:00 | Month's progress vs plan; [[founder-mindset]] self-check (top 2 priorities, assumptions, fast decisions, pivots); job-search pipeline | `reviews/monthly/YYYY-MM.md` |
| **Quarterly** | 1 Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct, 11:00 | Market review routine ([[coaching-approach]]); base vs worst signals ([[future-timeline]]); verdict: **full speed / adjust / revise** | `reviews/quarterly/YYYY-Qn.md` |
| **Annual** | 2 Jan, 10:00 | Deep strategy review: the year against [[action-plan]]; refresh [[ai-vision]] implications, timeline and 1/3/5-year plan | `reviews/annual/YYYY.md` |

## Weekly plan format
- **Last week:** done / not done / why.
- **This week:** a bold heading per day with total hours, then **one bullet per action** (never several actions on one line), each tied to an [[action-plan]] item, with a time estimate. **The first bullet each day is a commute reading assignment**, per the day's theme in [[reading-list]] and the current [[learning-plans]] week; it sits outside the 20 h. **Include the link** whenever the reading has one (the specific article if known, otherwise the source's homepage). Whenever the user asks for their weekly plan in chat, show the reading items and links too. Saturday's review asks for one takeaway per read and logs it in [[reading-list]].
- **Timeline:** on track / behind / ahead for each Year-1 checkpoint.
- **New thoughts:** each one gets logged in the right note.

## Verdict rule (monthly and up)
- **Full speed:** checkpoints on track and signals at base case.
- **Adjust:** one checkpoint slipping, or one signal moving toward the worst case.
- **Revise:** two or more checkpoints missed, worst-case signals firing, or a key fact changed. Re-run [[specialism-decision]] logic.
