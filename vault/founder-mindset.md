# Founder Mindset

The traits I want to embody. Links: [[index]], [[career-direction]], [[decision-log]].

The three fit together. **Vision** chooses the arena, **focus** picks the one or two bets, and **speed** executes and pivots when the evidence changes.

## 1. Speed
**Definition:** make decisions quickly, learn from mistakes, act without perfect information, and pivot as soon as the evidence says the direction is wrong.

- Set a decision deadline for every open choice. Example: 48 hours for reversible decisions.
- Sort decisions into two types:
  - Reversible ("two-way door"): decide fast.
  - Irreversible: slow down and gather evidence.
- Ship a small version of any new idea within one week.
- Signal of progress: the gap between idea and first action keeps shrinking, and pivots happen early.
- Failure mode: moving fast in the wrong direction, with no review loop.

## 2. Focus
**Definition:** identify the top one or two things that matter, ignore the rest, and periodically check that the assumptions behind the strategy still hold.

- At the start of each month, write the top two priorities in the decision log.
- Keep a stop list of three things I am explicitly not doing.
- Monthly "look up" review: list the conditions that justified the plan, and mark which still hold.
- Failure mode: tunnel vision, or the opposite problem of too many parallel bets.

## 3. Vision
**Definition:** see where the market is heading in the next 6-12 months, and roughly where it will be in 5-10 years. Use that to choose focus and to know where to move fast.

- Track three signals each quarter. Candidates: hiring trends, tool adoption, investment themes.
- Keep a one-page 6-12 month outlook and a 5-10 year sketch. Review both quarterly.
- For each claim, record the evidence, the date, and whether it is a prediction to test.
- Failure mode: a vision that is never tested against reality.
