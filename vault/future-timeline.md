# Future Timeline: 2027-2036

Links: [[index]], [[ai-vision]], [[ai-maturity-jobs]], [[future-of-work]], [[career-direction]], [[specialism-decision]].

*Scenarios, not forecasts. The base case follows [[ai-vision]]. The worst case is the faster, harsher version. Built from [[future-of-work]] and [[ai-maturity-jobs]] evidence plus my estimates. Review quarterly against the signals.*

## Base vs worst: the difference
- **Base:** AI capability keeps improving, but adoption is slowed by regulation, integration and organisational inertia. Displacement comes in waves, triggered by recessions.
- **Worst:** agents become reliable for most knowledge work by ~2028, and a recession lets firms cut deeply at once. Policy lags.

## 1 year (end 2027)
| | Base | Worst |
|---|---|---|
| Jobs | Entry-level hiring freeze deepens in tech, BA, junior analyst and back office. Seniors keep jobs but teams shrink through attrition. | Recession triggers AI-justified layoffs (Goldman: ~1 in 11 workers at risk, arriving with a downturn). Mid-level knowledge workers cut too. |
| Finance / HK | Banks run multi-year cuts (HSBC ~10% over 3-5 years), mainly in ops and the middle office. Every business line has "AI lead" roles, filled by seniors. | HSBC accelerates the cuts. Global service centres are hit hard; HK middle office shrinks quickly. |
| Industries | AI vendors, data centres and energy grow. BPO, IT services and agencies get squeezed. | IT services and outsourcing contract sharply. |
| Me | Raise and role in the sponsor's team. Build an outside option. | Outside option is critical; runway (~3 years of cash) matters. |

## 10 years (end 2036)
| | Base | Worst |
|---|---|---|
| Jobs | Work = ownership, accountability, trust, judgment and scarce context. Many people sit in category 7 (low-pay human services) or on transfers. | Labour income collapses for most people. Returns flow almost entirely to capital owners. |
| Economy | New social contract emerging (AI taxes, public dividends). | Severe inequality. Owning assets is the main way to have income. |
| Me | Own capital and/or a business. Hold accountable roles. | Capital ownership decides outcomes. Assets matter more than salary. |

## Cross-scenario conclusions
1. **Both scenarios end in the same place.** Only the speed differs. Plan for the worst-case timing and enjoy the base case if it comes.
2. **Build capital now.** In both cases, asset ownership becomes the main source of income by 2036. Salary is a temporary tool to buy assets ([[investing]]).
3. **The 2027-2029 window matters most:** move into accountable, business-side, relationship-heavy positions while hiring still exists.
4. **Runway is strategic.** ~3 years of cash protects me through a worst-case recession.
